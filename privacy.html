Privacy Policy

Arscor Distribution Engine — Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Who we are

Arscor Distribution Engine ("the Service") is an internal content distribution tool operated by Arscor, used by authorized team members to publish Arscor's own video content to social media platforms. Contact: tech@arscor.io.

2. Information we collect

The Service collects only the minimum data needed to operate:

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) granted by the connected social media accounts, used exclusively to publish content and refresh authorization.
  • Basic account identifiers provided by each platform (such as an account/open ID and display name), used to identify which account a scheduled post belongs to.
  • Content metadata (video files, titles, and scheduling information) uploaded by Arscor's own team for publication.

The Service does not collect personal data from the general public, does not track visitors, and does not use cookies or analytics.

3. How we use information

Data is used solely to schedule and publish Arscor's content to the connected platforms and to keep authorizations valid (token refresh). We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, other than transmitting content to the platform selected for publication via its official API.

4. Data storage and security

Tokens and account records are stored in a private, access-controlled database. Credentials are never published or shared. Access is limited to authorized Arscor personnel.

5. Data retention and revocation

Tokens are retained only while an account remains connected. Account holders can revoke the Service's access at any time from the connected platform's security settings (e.g., TikTok's "Manage app permissions"), after which the stored tokens become invalid and are deactivated. Stored data can be deleted on request to tech@arscor.io.

6. Third-party platforms

Content published through the Service is subject to the privacy policy of the destination platform (e.g., TikTok, YouTube, X). We encourage reviewing those policies directly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
